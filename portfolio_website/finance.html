Project Case Study

Finance Website

A client-facing financial brand website delivered through a cross-functional team, focused on clarity, trust, and practical business value.

  • Role: Developer
  • Type: Client Project
  • Context: CMPT 375
  • Stack: Django, Python, HTML, CSS, JavaScript
  • Duration: Full Semester

Project Preview

Overview

The site was built to support a financial advisor’s online presence, communicate services clearly, and present a professional experience for prospective clients.

What I Learned

I learned how software design changes when a real client, brand expectations, and team coordination all shape the final product, not just the technical implementation.

What I Accomplished

I contributed to a polished, client-facing website that supported real business goals while meeting team expectations for quality, presentation, and iterative delivery.

Comp Sci Learning Goals

  • Understood software design by building a practical web product shaped by client requirements and team workflow.
  • Acquired significant project experience in a group setting through collaboration with developers, designers, and stakeholders.
  • Developed effective problem solving skills by balancing technical scope, changing requests, and production quality.

University Wide Leaning Goals

  • Critical Thinking: weighed user needs, business goals, and technical constraints throughout the project.
  • Creativity: helped shape a professional digital experience around branding and trust.
  • Collaboration: worked as part of a cross-functional team with shared ownership of delivery.
  • Communication: relied on clear team discussion and client feedback to guide implementation.
  • Global Responsibility: built for a real audience, which raised the standard for reliability, clarity, and professionalism.

Execution Timeline

The project ran across a full semester with iterative planning, feedback loops, and phased releases.

Primary Goal

Create a polished, client-ready digital presence for a real business.

Notable Challenge

Balancing technical scope with evolving client preferences and deadlines.

Key Outcome

Stronger experience delivering production-minded work with stakeholders.
